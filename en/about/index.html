About

We are an independent, small mobile app studio.

Where the name comes from

Yelkovan is the Turkish word for the minute hand of a clock — the faster one, turning all day without stopping. Look at a clock and it is the only hand you can see moving.

It is also the name of a seabird that flies over the Bosphorus in long formations and almost never settles. Both meanings land in the same place: the thing that keeps going.

How we work

We are a small team and we do the whole job ourselves — design, development, store releases and maintenance. Our apps are written in Flutter, so a single codebase ships to both iOS and Android. Firebase handles the server side.

On AI, let us be plain: we do not train our own foundation models. We build on models from Anthropic and OpenAI. Our work is turning those models into a product experience that holds up — deciding what to ask them, how to present the result to a child or a student, and when not to use them at all.

Three principles

  • Claims you can check. Every number on this site and on our store listings is real and carries a date. We do not publish inflated user counts or ratings that cannot be verified.
  • Child safety is not up for discussion. Faby has no advertising, no third-party tracking and no free-form chat. These are boundaries, not revenue lines.
  • We prefer ad-free products. We would rather earn from the product itself than from selling our users' attention.
