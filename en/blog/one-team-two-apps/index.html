One team, two apps

19 September 2026Yelkovan5 min

We have two products and they have nothing in common. Faby reads a bedtime story to a four-year-old; Sorula analyses a seventeen-year-old's mock exam. They nonetheless sit on the same technical base: Flutter and Firebase. Not because those are "the best" tools, but because a small team running two products at once needs a single mental model.

What one codebase buys you

The real benefit of Flutter in practice is not "write once, run anywhere". It is this: when you fix a bug you have fixed it on both platforms, and you never have to work out why the two platforms behave differently. In a small team, time goes into "why does it do that here" far more than into writing new features. Not doubling that question is a serious win.

Where you pay for it

You start paying the moment you need something platform-specific. A parent recording their own voice in Faby, reading a question through the camera in Sorula — both drop into the native layer, and there Flutter does not help you, it just gets out of the way. That work takes longer than you expect. It belongs in its own line when you plan.

What we learned about AI

We do not train the models in either product; we use existing ones. The hard part is not picking a model, it is deciding when not to use one. When a student points a camera at a maths question in Sorula, a correct answer is not enough — the solution has to be broken into steps the student can follow. In Faby, a beautiful story is not enough — it has to fit the age group, and the same character has to look the same on every page.

None of those constraints is solved by model quality. They are solved on the product side, by designing what to ask, how to present the result, and what never to show at all.

In short

For a small team the right question is not "which technology is best" but "which technology surprises me in the fewest places". Keeping both products on one base means everything we learn applies to both. The price is moving slowly on platform-specific work. For now that trade still makes sense to us.
