What it does

The child is the hero

Their name, their look and the theme they pick put them in the lead role. Nine themes and six illustration styles to choose from.

From drawing to story

The child draws on the in-app canvas, and Faby reads that drawing and builds a story around it. The drawing itself appears on the book's cover.

Read it in your own voice

Six narrator voices are included. You can also record your own voice and have the stories read to your child in it.

Seven languages

Turkish, English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese and Hindi — each written for that language rather than translated into it.

Fifteen world classics

From Cinderella to Rapunzel, fifteen classic tales in all seven languages, free for good.

More than reading

Every page carries a dictionary, a quiz, a jigsaw and a colouring layer. When the story ends there is still something to do.

From the app

Why we built it

We built Faby so that children would come away from screen time with something. A child watching ready-made content and a child building their own story — choosing a character, answering questions, drawing a picture — do not have the same relationship with the screen.

We designed the app around that: every story is made for that particular child, and every story leaves something to do at the end. Parents get a weekly report showing what was read and which words came up.

Child safety

  • No advertising. No banners, no rewarded video. In a product made for children this is not negotiable for us.
  • No third-party tracking. We run no external analytics tools inside the app.
  • No free text, no chat. The friends feature works with preset nicknames and emoji reactions; children cannot send each other free-form messages.
  • Parental gate. The profile and store sections sit behind parent verification when a younger age range is set.
  • No real children in our marketing. We use only illustrations and app screens.
