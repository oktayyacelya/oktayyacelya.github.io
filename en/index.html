How we work

Small team, end to end

We do all of it ourselves. Flutter and Firebase let us ship both iOS and Android from a single codebase.

No ads, no tracking

We use no third-party analytics in our apps. Faby carries no advertising at all — in a product made for children, that is not negotiable for us.

We turn AI into products

We do not train our own foundation models. We build on models from Anthropic and OpenAI, and our work is turning them into experiences that are genuinely useful day to day.
