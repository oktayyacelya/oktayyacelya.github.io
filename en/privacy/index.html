Privacy

This site collects nothing about you.

Last updated: September 2026

This website

  • No cookies. Which is why you are not being asked to accept any.
  • No visitor analytics. There is no Google Analytics or anything like it on this site. We do not know how many people visit.
  • No forms. There is nothing here for you to fill in, so you send us no data.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, like any web server, keeps technical access logs. We have no access to them.

If you email us

Your address and what you write are used only to reply to you. You are not added to a mailing list and nothing is shared with third parties.

Our apps are separate

This page covers the yelkovan.ai website only. Faby and Sorula have their own privacy policies; account, progress and purchase data is handled inside those apps.

You can find those policies on the apps' store listings and their own sites: fabyfun.com ↗ · sorula.app ↗
