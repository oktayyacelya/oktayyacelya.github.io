What it does

Nineteen exams, one app

Turkey's university entrance exams (TYT, AYT), secondary school placement (LGS), civil service (KPSS), language proficiency (YDS, TOEFL, IELTS), postgraduate and professional exams, plus grade-level tracks.

Question generation and solving

Pick a topic and a difficulty, and a test is generated. Point the camera at a question you are stuck on and it is worked through step by step.

PDF into a test

Upload a PDF and the app reads it and turns the content into a test you can actually sit.

Past paper simulator

Past exams from 2018 to 2025, in their real format and under their real time limits.

Mistake log and topic map

Wrong answers collect in one place and your weak topics are mapped. Weekly reports and a progress card come out of it.

Quiz rooms

Sit the same test as your friends at the same time with a live scoreboard. A university preference advisor built on YÖK Atlas data is included too.

From the app

Why we built it

Exam preparation in Turkey is a scattered process: the question bank is in one place, the mock exams in another, and tracking what you actually do not know is nowhere. A student moves between half a dozen tools a day and none of them knows about the others.

We built Sorula to pull that into one app. That is where the "super app" decision came from — question generation, solving, mock exams, progress tracking and university choice in the same place, so each can learn from the others. The app was developed during the İTÜ Çekirdek accelerator programme.
